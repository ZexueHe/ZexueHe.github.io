Research Highlights

My research builds long-context foundation models (LLMs/VLMs) and long-horizon agents that can reliably reason and act over extended contexts. I push the capability frontier of LLMs via scalable synthetic data methods and modeling and training approaches,

  • Synthetic Training/Data for Foundation Models
    Synthetic SFT [TDG];
    Synthetic Pretraining [for Data Scarcity] and Mid-training [ for Long-context Extension];
    Synthetic RL [COGS];
    Synthetic Tool Generation for Agentic Reasoning [SWE Bandit].
  • Long-Context LLMs and Stateful Agents
    Efficient memory mechanisms [M+, CAMELoT];
    multi-step stateful agent under partial observable environments [POMDP],
    and long-horizon decision-making [MemoryArena, Decocted Experience].
  • LLM Safety, Alignment and Evaluation
    Preference alignment and safety [Granite Vision, Detoxification, WildFeedback] and stress testing [BiasFreeBench, CogBias].
